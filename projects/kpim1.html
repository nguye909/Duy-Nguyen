The Problem & Context

What decision needed to be made?

Retail transaction data can show what was sold without clearly explaining why performance differs across stores, districts, products, or customer groups. This project brought those views together to answer which areas contribute the most revenue, whether targets are being met, where customer retention might need attention, and how orders progress from placement to shipment and payment.

My Role

What I owned

I developed the Power BI reporting experience using Power Query, a relational data model, and DAX measures. I built KPI definitions, revenue and target comparisons, time-based performance analysis, RFM customer scores, and order tracking views. I also designed slicers and bookmark navigation so viewers could move between summary metrics and operational details.

Methodology

How I approached the analysis

STEP 01

Prepare the model and define KPIs

Worked with sales transactions and related customer, product, store, promotion, calendar, and sales-plan tables. Created reusable DAX for revenue, distinct orders, sales targets, year-over-year growth, and order performance.

STEP 02

Analyze drivers and customer behavior

Compared performance by location, manager, product, promotion, and reporting period. Used recency, frequency, and monetary scores to classify customer behavior and explore segments alongside demographic characteristics.

STEP 03

Build interactive report views

Organized four report pages with KPI cards, trends, matrices, decomposition analysis, and bookmark-controlled chart switching for executive summaries and more detailed investigation.

Dashboard Breakdown

What the four Power BI views analyze

Rather than treating revenue as a single KPI, the report links financial results to customer segments and the underlying order process.

01 / Revenue Analysis

Where revenue comes from

The executive view summarizes revenue, orders, customers, and units sold across the reporting period. District and store comparisons highlight geographic contributions, while product-category and product-group charts show the composition of sales.

Yearly revenue trends and manager/product drilldowns help distinguish overall growth from performance concentrated in specific locations or categories.

02 / Sales Performance

Actual results versus plan

Monthly and year-to-date KPIs compare realized revenue with sales targets. Trend charts place actual revenue alongside target and prior-year results; manager and district comparisons identify where the largest gaps occur.

A store-by-month matrix supports follow-up on target attainment rather than relying only on company-wide performance.

03 / Customer Analysis

RFM segmentation and customer behavior

Customer recency, purchase frequency, and monetary value are scored and combined into RFM segments. The view compares customer groups and explores demographics such as income and age through a decomposition tree.

Customer-level details provide last-purchase date, purchase count, average purchase value, and total spend to support more targeted analysis.

04 / Order Analysis

From order placement to payment

The order view brings together order volume, units per order, average order value, and shipping/payment indicators. Bookmark buttons switch the trend analysis between order quantity, paid orders, outstanding orders, and order value.

Manager and product-category breakdowns help reveal where orders remain unshipped or shipped but unpaid. Payment dates are used as a completion proxy because no separate fulfillment-confirmation field is shown in the source data.

Key Findings

What I concluded from the analysis

Finding 01

December 2022 exceeded its revenue target

December revenue reached approximately VND 698.1 million against a VND 544.4 million target, or 128.22% attainment. Revenue for 2022 reached roughly VND 5.90 billion, equal to 105.24% of the annual plan. The monthly result was strong, but the year-to-date comparison provides a more balanced assessment of performance.

Finding 02

Revenue was concentrated in a few locations and product categories

Across the full 2020–2022 Revenue Analysis view, Cầu Giấy accounted for 33.67% of revenue, followed by Hai Bà Trưng at 18.69% and Ba Đình at 12.52%. Household goods represented 86.01% of revenue in that same view. These concentrations make district-level and category-level performance important to monitor.

Finding 03

RFM highlights customer retention as an area for investigation

In the displayed Customer Analysis view, the At Risk group represents approximately 77% of customers. This suggests an opportunity to examine purchase recency and re-engagement patterns; the segment score thresholds should be validated before using the classification for campaigns.

Business Value

How the dashboard supports decisions

Management can compare sales performance against plan, identify high-contributing stores and categories, explore customer segments, and investigate order activity without switching between separate spreadsheets. Slicers and drilldowns make it easier to move from company-level KPIs to a specific operational question.

Outcome / Achievement

What the project produced

A four-page, interactive Power BI report integrating revenue analysis, target attainment, RFM customer segmentation, and order analysis. The project demonstrates data modeling, DAX development, financial KPI design, and the communication of business results through dashboard storytelling.
